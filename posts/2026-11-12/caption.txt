Amazon UK shoppers can now see a full year of your price history in one tap.

Amazon launched Alexa for Shopping in the UK on 11 November. It's on the Amazon app and website, free for any signed-in customer, and you don't need Prime or an Echo to use it.

The bit I'd pay attention to as a seller: any shopper can tap "Price History" on a product page and see how the price has moved over 30, 60 or 365 days.

With Black Friday Week starting 19 November, that matters. If your deal price isn't really lower than what you've charged recently, shoppers can now see that for themselves.

It also adds AI summaries to search results and product pages, plus side-by-side comparisons of features, prices and review highlights. So clear bullet points and solid reviews are doing even more work for you.

This week I'd open your best sellers in the app, check the price history as a shopper would, and ask Alexa to compare your product with a competitor's. You'll learn a lot from what it says.
